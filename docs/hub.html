Preregistrations and Merged Work

Gemma Ball Preregistration

PR #47 landed the preregistration for the integer ball on Gemma, smallest models first, before any Gemma weight passed through the ball. It is now at experiments/model-output-gemma-ball-2026-11-23. Results from that sweep are on main; the preregistered primary Gemma 4 E4B BF16 sweep is still outstanding.

Hadamard-Turned E8

PR #41 is merged and its turned E8 record is on main. Still open: a packed format and decoder for the turned lattice, the Voronoi arms on Llama-3.2, and Gemma-2B.

Limits

  • Decode is slow. A packed ball file decodes at 20 to 25 million weights a second on one core, about a minute for a model. The file is decoded to floats first, so nothing is saved when the model runs. No run-time saving yet. Source: packed-ball-format-2026-11-18.
  • The Gemma 4 E4B bridge has a limit. Its two E8 Voronoi arms were scored against Q8_0, not BF16, with no stock comparison arm. The preregistered BF16 sweep is still outstanding. The E8 headline above uses an 8-bit reference, not the weights as trained.
  • The zero codeword is model-dependent. On Qwen2.5 a codeword at zero helps E8; on SmolLM2 it does not. The two models disagree about the centre.
  • The headline line fails on the third model. With no matrix on either side, the ball arm is behind Q4_K_M on Llama-3.2: 0.0478 against 0.0432 on wikitext. With a matrix it is far ahead, and with ten loud columns left whole it is ahead with no data at all. Source: third-model-llama32-2026-11-18.
  • Accuracy is a bound, not a win. On the MMLU subset the ball arms sit within 0.70 points of the 16-bit model, and the run can only resolve a loss of 1.1 to 1.7 points. Source: mmlu-subset-2026-11-18.
