Limits

  • Real-weight KL is measured on wikitext-2 test text only, with no calibration data. Other kinds of text are not yet tested for PR #41 arms.
  • No packed format or decoder yet exists for the turned E8 lattice. Its bit counts are counted, not stored.
  • Roofline figures are bandwidth ceilings. Measured kernel tokens per second are not in the record.
  • On CPU, every E8 arm is far slower than the Q4_K_M baseline. The records state this and do not claim otherwise.
