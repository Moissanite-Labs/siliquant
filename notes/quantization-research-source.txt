1. The 2025–2026 Quantization Frontier and Where Your Research Fits

Competitive sub-4-bit LLM compression pipelines in 2025–2026 predominantly follow a two-stage paradigm: applying a function-preserving linear transform to reshape weight and activation distributions, then rounding onto a scalar LUT, trellis, or high-dimensional lattice codebook.
